Grown under the tablecloth

Cool-climate wine from the Constantia slopes, made in the shade of Table Mountain's famous cloud.

The wind does half the work

Most summer afternoons, the south-easter drags a cloud over Table Mountain. Capetonians call it the tablecloth.

From the city it looks like a postcard. From our rows on the far side of the ridge it's a cold front that arrives at two o'clock and stays until dark. Our grapes ripen slowly, keep their acidity, and taste of the place they grew.
