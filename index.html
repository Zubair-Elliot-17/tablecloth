About Tablecloth

We are a family wine farm in Constantia, on the far side of Table Mountain from the city. Vines have grown on this slope since 1891.

Most summer afternoons the south-easter drags a cloud over the top of the mountain. From town it looks like a postcard. From our rows it's a cold front that arrives at about two o'clock and stays until dark, so the vineyard runs around seven degrees cooler than the city. The grapes ripen slowly and keep their acidity.

Everything happens on the farm. We grow 34 hectares of vines, pick them by hand at dawn in February and March, make about 60,000 bottles a year in the cellar at the bottom of the slope, and pour them at the cellar door six days a week.

Where each wine grows

We don't buy in grapes. Each wine comes from its own block on the farm, picked by hand and driven a few hundred metres down to the cellar.
